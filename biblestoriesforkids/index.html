Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short

  • No accounts. You never sign up or log in.
  • No ads and no tracking. No advertising, analytics or tracking tools are in the app.
  • No personal data. We don't ask for names, email addresses, photos, location or contacts, and we don't collect them.
  • Your child's reading stays on your device. Progress, favourites and settings are saved only on this phone or tablet.

What the app stores on your device

Reading progress, favourite stories and prayers, and your settings (language, text size, music, sleep timer) are saved on the device so the app can remember them. They are never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Story packs are one-time purchases made through the App Store or Google Play. To check which packs you own, the app uses a service called RevenueCat. It receives the store's purchase receipt and a random, anonymous ID created by the app. It does not receive your name, email or payment details. We use this only to unlock the packs you bought and to restore them. Apple and Google handle payment under their own privacy policies.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children aged 3 to 10 and their families. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchases, restoring purchases and links that leave the app are behind a grown-up check.

Sharing

We don't sell or share any information. The only outside services involved are the app stores and RevenueCat, for purchases, as described above.

Your choices

You can delete all data on the device by deleting the app. To ask about the anonymous purchase record, contact us and we will help, for example by deleting it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the policy in the app's store listing.
